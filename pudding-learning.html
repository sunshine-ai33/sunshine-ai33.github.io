🎯 Focus: Sentence Building

14 Lessons (25 minutes per lesson)

1️⃣ Likes, Dislikes, and Feelings

  • I am.... (happy, tired, sad)
  • I like.... (playing, reading, playing sports)
  • I don't like.... (eating vegetables, the hot weather)

2️⃣ Want and Need + Reason (because)

  • I want.... because....
  • I need.... because....

3️⃣ Identifying and Locating Things/Objects

  • It is a.... / This is a....
  • It is in/on/at....
